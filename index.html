Good to know

Straight answers, so you know what to expect.

Is what I share confidential?

Yes. Your conversations are confidential, and I don’t share what you tell me. The one exception is safety: if I believe you or someone else is in immediate danger, I may contact emergency services.

Who are sessions for?

My sessions and groups are for adults 18 and older.

Is this therapy?

No. Vent & Repent Studios offers listening and support. It isn’t therapy, medical care, or a crisis service, and it doesn’t replace a licensed professional.

What if I’m in crisis?

Please reach out for urgent help. In the US, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), or call 911 in an emergency.

How do I cancel or reschedule?

Let me know at least 24 hours before your time and I’ll gladly move it. Sessions cancelled with less notice, or missed, may not be refundable, though I’ll always try to be understanding.

What about refunds?

If I have to cancel, you’ll get a full refund or a new time, whichever you prefer. If something wasn’t right, tell me and we’ll work it out.

How do memberships work?

Memberships renew monthly. Your included sessions are for that month and expire when it ends. You can cancel anytime. Just tell me before your next month begins. Membership payments are sent monthly through Venmo.

What do I need for a session?

A phone, tablet, or computer with a camera, an internet connection, and a quiet spot where you feel comfortable. Sessions are held by video call.

How fast will you reply?

I aim to reply to calls, texts, and emails within 24 hours.

I’m so glad you’re here

This is where Vent & Repent Studios comes from.

I was born in a small town called Jarra, in The Gambia. Where I grew up, poverty was a struggle, and every day felt like a battle.

I wasn’t the only one. Across the country, more than half of all people were living through the same thing.

53.4%of people in The Gambia were living below the national poverty line in 2020, according to the World Bank. Read the source

What got me through was my mom. When things got too heavy, she would comfort me and tell me to be brave. That meant everything. It told me someone believed in me.

Be brave. Push through. You can do anything you put your mind to.What my mom told me

I’ve carried those words with me ever since. And I learned something else: heavy things get lighter when someone really listens. Sometimes being heard is what lets you keep going.

That’s why I started Vent & Repent Studios. I know what it’s like to carry a lot, and I don’t want anyone to feel like they have to carry it alone.

What you can expect from me

  • No judgment, ever
  • That I’ll listen first, with my full attention
  • No rushing. Take the time you need
  • A reminder that you’re stronger than you think

I can’t promise to fix everything. What I can promise is that I’ll listen, I’ll treat what you share with care, and I’ll remind you, like my mom reminded me, that you’re braver than you know.

With care,
your host at Vent & Repent Studios
